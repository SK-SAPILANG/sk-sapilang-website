About SK Sapilang

Youth Leadership. Community Impact.

The Sangguniang Kabataan of Barangay Sapilang serves as the official youth governance body of Barangay Sapilang, Bacnotan, La Union—advancing meaningful youth participation, responsive programs, transparent governance, and opportunities that empower young people to become active partners in community development.

Institutional Profile

Serving the youth with purpose.

Sangguniang Kabataan of Barangay Sapilang

The Sangguniang Kabataan of Barangay Sapilang is the youth governing body representing the Katipunan ng Kabataan within the barangay. It provides young people with a formal platform for participation in local governance, leadership, community service, policy development and youth-centered initiatives.

SK Sapilang implements programs and services in education, health and well-being, sports and recreation, leadership development, environmental action, digital access, community engagement and other areas responsive to the needs and aspirations of the youth.

Youth Governance Mandate

Meaningful youth participation in local governance.

The Sangguniang Kabataan operates under Republic Act No. 10742, or the Sangguniang Kabataan Reform Act of 2015, as amended by Republic Act No. 11768. It provides a mechanism for meaningful youth participation in governance and supports the formulation and implementation of plans, programs and policies responsive to youth development.
