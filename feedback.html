Quality Cycle

From feedback to improvement.

The system follows a simple quality cycle that helps transform youth feedback into actionable service improvements.

01

Collect

Gather feedback, evaluations, suggestions and concerns from youth and stakeholders.

02

Evaluate

Review satisfaction scores, comments, concerns and recurring service issues.

03

Improve

Identify necessary adjustments to programs, services, systems and implementation.

04

Monitor

Use future feedback to determine whether improvements are effective and sustainable.

Seminar / Training / Activity Evaluation Form

Use this form after participating in an SK seminar, training, workshop, orientation, sports activity, youth assembly, community activity, advocacy program, competition or other SK-led initiative.

Overall Activity Rating *

Rate your overall experience from 1 to 5.

Detailed Evaluation

Rate each aspect of the seminar, training or activity.

How useful, appropriate and connected the topic or activity was to the needs, interests and development of the participants.
How well the seminar, training or activity achieved its stated purpose, expected outcomes and learning objectives.
Evaluate the speaker's knowledge of the topic, clarity of presentation, communication skills, preparedness, responsiveness to questions and ability to engage participants.
How organized and smoothly implemented the activity was, including registration, instructions, coordination, flow of activities and assistance from organizers.
How suitable, accessible, comfortable, safe and appropriate the venue and facilities were for the activity.
The quality, availability and usefulness of presentation materials, handouts, equipment, supplies and other resources used during the activity.
How effectively the schedule was followed and whether enough time was provided for discussions, activities, questions and other important parts of the program.
How effectively the activity encouraged interaction, participation, questions, collaboration and active involvement from participants.

Resource Speaker / Trainer / Facilitator Feedback

Provide additional feedback about the person who delivered or facilitated the session. If the activity had no resource speaker, you may write “Not Applicable.”

How knowledgeable, credible and confident the resource speaker appeared in discussing the subject matter.
How clearly the speaker explained ideas, used understandable examples and communicated information to participants.
How effectively the speaker encouraged participation, maintained interest and created opportunities for questions or interaction.
How well the speaker listened to questions and provided helpful, understandable and relevant responses.
